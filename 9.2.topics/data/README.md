# Headline dataset

64 original, synthetic English headlines written for this activity. They are
fictional teaching examples, not quotations from a news outlet or reports of
actual events. The CSV has one `headline` column and no topic labels.

Repeated vocabulary makes topic words visible on a small corpus; several
headlines deliberately mix subjects. This is a simplified teaching dataset,
not a representative sample of real news. No download, account, or external
license is required. Preserve the supplied file. This is only the Part 1 reference corpus; students
find a different dataset for Part 2 and document its source and access.

Short headlines give LDA limited co-occurrence evidence. Do not treat the
reference topics as a benchmark or as a representative model of real news.
