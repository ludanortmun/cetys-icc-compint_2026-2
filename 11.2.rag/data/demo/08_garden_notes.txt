Campus garden volunteers — unrelated notice

The campus garden volunteers are a separate fictional group and are not part of the Orion Makers Club. Garden volunteers meet on Saturdays at 09:00 beside the greenhouse. Their coordinator is Paula Méndez. Their schedule does not change the makers club's Thursday meetings.

This month's garden task is to label seed trays and check the drip irrigation lines. Volunteers should bring a hat and closed shoes. Gloves are available from the greenhouse cabinet. Garden tools remain at the greenhouse and are not borrowed through the makers club's equipment ledger.

The garden group plans to grow basil, mint, and cherry tomatoes. Basil seedlings need consistent moisture but should not sit in standing water. The group records watering observations in a notebook kept near the greenhouse entrance. These observations are garden records, not laboratory safety instructions or project reimbursement policies.
