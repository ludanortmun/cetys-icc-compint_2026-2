Orion Makers Club — meetings and workshops

Regular Orion Makers Club meetings take place every Thursday at 16:30 in room B-204. A regular meeting starts with ten minutes of announcements, followed by project work and a closing cleanup. Meetings end at 18:00. The room is not available to the club on Friday evenings.

The introductory soldering workshop takes place on November 19 at 15:00 in the electronics laboratory, room C-110. Attendance is limited to twelve students. To reserve a place, write your name on the workshop desk's signup sheet before November 17. Participants must first complete the safety orientation. The workshop provides practice boards and soldering irons, so participants do not need to bring equipment.

The project showcase is scheduled for December 3 from 17:00 to 19:00 in the courtyard. Each team receives one table and access to a shared power strip. Teams must submit their table request to Maya Torres by November 26. A regular meeting is canceled on a day when the showcase is held.
