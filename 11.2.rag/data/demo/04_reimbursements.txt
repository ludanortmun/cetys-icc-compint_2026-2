Orion Makers Club — purchases and reimbursements

The club can reimburse approved project purchases up to 450 pesos per team per month. Approval from the treasurer, Inés Vega, must be obtained before buying materials. The limit applies to the entire team, not to each member. Purchases made without advance approval are not eligible.

To request reimbursement, give Inés Vega the original receipt, the project name, and a short description of how the materials will be used. Submit the request within seven calendar days after the purchase. Reimbursements are processed on the last Wednesday of the month. Keep a copy of the receipt for your team's records.

Food, personal clothing, and transportation are not reimbursable project materials. A separately approved field trip may have its own transport arrangement, but that does not change the project reimbursement rules. If a receipt includes both eligible components and personal items, clearly mark only the approved project components.
