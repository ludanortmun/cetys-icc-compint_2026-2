Orion Makers Club — equipment loans

Members may borrow a multimeter or a basic screwdriver set for up to three calendar days. A borrower signs the equipment ledger at the workshop desk and records the item number, checkout date, and expected return date. The equipment steward is Leo Ramos. The steward checks that returned equipment is complete and working.

Soldering stations, bench power supplies, and the laser cutter must stay inside the laboratory. They cannot be checked out overnight. Members may reserve these tools during supervised laboratory hours. A reservation is not permission to take equipment home.

If an item is damaged, stop using it and inform Leo Ramos immediately. Do not attempt a repair without authorization. Members who need a longer multimeter loan must ask the steward before the original deadline; extensions are not automatic. Personal laptops and phones are not club equipment and are not recorded in the equipment ledger.
