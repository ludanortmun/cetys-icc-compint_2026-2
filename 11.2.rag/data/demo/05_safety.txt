Orion Makers Club — laboratory safety

Every tool user must complete a thirty-minute safety orientation with the laboratory supervisor, Dr. Elena Cruz. Orientations are held on Tuesdays at 14:00 in room C-110. Completion is recorded in the safety register. Attending a club meeting does not count as completing orientation.

Wear safety glasses when soldering or cutting material, tie back long hair, and keep bags away from walkways. Never operate the laser cutter alone. Its operator and a trained observer must both be present. Only materials listed on the laboratory's approved-materials card may go into the laser cutter.

If you smell smoke or notice overheating, stop the tool and notify the supervisor. The emergency assembly point is the north courtyard. Food and drinks must remain outside the laboratory. At the end of a session, switch off equipment, return shared tools, and remove scraps from the workbench. Report incidents even if nobody was injured.
