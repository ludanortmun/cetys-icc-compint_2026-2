Orion Makers Club — science museum visit

The club's science museum visit is scheduled for November 28. Participants meet at the south campus gate at 08:15; the bus leaves at 08:30 and returns to campus at approximately 15:00. The destination is the fictional Harbor Science Museum. This trip does not use the regular meeting room.

There are twenty seats. Sign up at the workshop desk by November 20 and submit a signed participation form. The transport contribution is 80 pesos per participant. Museum admission is covered by the club. Bring a packed lunch and a refillable water bottle because lunch is not provided.

The trip coordinator is Daniel Soto. Notify him before November 24 if you need an accessibility arrangement. Participants who cancel should tell him promptly so a student on the waiting list can take the seat. The museum visit is independent of the December project showcase, and signing up for one does not register you for the other.
